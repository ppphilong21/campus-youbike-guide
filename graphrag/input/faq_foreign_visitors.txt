YouBike for foreign visitors, international students and one-time users

There are two ways for a foreign national to ride a YouBike in Taipei.

Option 1, become a member. Registration accepts a passport number or an ARC (residence certificate) number as well as a National ID number (Membership Agreement, Article 2.1). After registering, a foreign member rents with an IC card or a QR code like any other member.

Option 2, single rental without registering. Unregistered single rental users may rent and return a bike once by following the procedure in the app (Articles 1.4 and 5.7). The steps are:
1. Download the YouBike app (YouBike2.0) from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the side menu and tap "Login / Register", then tap "Single Rental" at the bottom.
3. Link a credit card and complete the email verification. The confirmation email includes the account details, the card number, the authorization period and the pre-authorization information.
4. Return to the home screen, tap "Scan" and scan the QR code on the bike's screen.
5. When the screen shows "Pull the bike backwards", pull the bike back to release it.
Public bicycle injury insurance enrollment must be in place before riding.

Cost of a single rental in Taipei City: the base rates apply, paid by credit card. The card is pre-authorized for 120 hours at a time with a NT$3,000 deposit, which is refunded after a normal return, subject to the card issuer's processing time.

The Taipei City FAQ has a separate category, "Foreign Nationals on Rentals & Single Rental (Temporary Member)", including a question on which credit card types can be used for single rentals.
