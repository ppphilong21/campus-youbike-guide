Management Building (管理大樓)
Type: academic building, number 10 on the campus map, building code MA.
Location: MA is part of the EE-MA-T2 complex on the Lane 41 side of campus. It is the wing facing the central lawn, connected to EE at the Alley 68 end and to T2 through a middle link.
Units in Management Building:
- College of Management (管理學院)
- Department of Industrial Management (工業管理系)
- Department of Business Administration (企業管理系)
- Department of Information Management (資訊管理系)
- Graduate Institute of Management (管理研究所)
