Second Academic Building (第二教學大樓)
Type: academic building, number 12 on the campus map, building code T2.
Location: T2 runs along Lane 41 and forms the Lane 41 side of the EE-MA-T2 complex. The T2 Cafeteria is in the courtyard between MA and T2. Taipei Municipal Minzu Junior High School is across Lane 41.
Units in Second Academic Building:
- College of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (電資學院)
