Administration Building (行政大樓)
Type: administration building, number 1 on the campus map, building code AD.
Location: Walking in from the Main Gate on Keelung Road, AD is the large building straight ahead. The Library and the RB building are on the left of the entrance path, the IB building is on the right, and the T4 building is further to the left. Lawns surround it.
Units in Administration Building:
- Office of the President (校長室)
- Office of the Executive Vice President (副校長室)
- Secretarial Office (秘書室)
- Accounting Office (主計室)
- Personnel Office (人事室)
- Office of Academic Affairs (教務處)
- Office of General Affairs (總務處)
- University Archives (校史室)
