Engineering Building 2 (工程二館)
Type: academic building, number 9 on the campus map, building code E2.
Location: E2 is a long building on the Lane 41 side of campus, in front of the EE-MA-T2 complex and behind the AD building. A car park sign and a security post are at its Lane 41 end.
Units in Engineering Building 2:
- Department of Chemical Engineering (化學工程系)
- Department of Civil and Construction Engineering (營建工程系)
