Third Academic Building (第三教學大樓)
Type: administration building, number 5 on the campus map, building code T3.
Location: T3 is a small building towards the Alley 68 side, between International House 2 and the E1 building and behind the Student Activity Center. The dormitory-side Back Gate is just beyond it.
Units in Third Academic Building:
- Property Management Division (總務處保管組)
- Documentation Division (總務處檔案室)
