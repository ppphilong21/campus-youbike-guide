TR Building (研揚大樓)
Type: academic building, number 15 on the campus map, building code TR.
Location: TR is a narrow building along Lane 155 at the Keelung Road corner of campus, beside the Athletic Field. The Lane 155 parking lot and a security post are at its end, and the NTU Veterinary Hospital is across Lane 155.
Units in TR Building:
- College of Applied Sciences (應用科技學院)
- Graduate Institute of Applied Science and Technology (應用科技研究所)
- Graduate Institute of Biomedical Engineering (醫學工程研究所)
- Graduate Institute of Patent (專利研究所)
- Bachelor's Program of Applied Science and Technology (應用科技學士學位學程)
- Inter-Disciplinary Bachelor's Program (不分系學士班)
- Graduate Institute of Technology Management (科技管理研究所)
- Hsinchu Campus Office (新竹分部辦公室)
