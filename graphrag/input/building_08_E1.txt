Engineering Building 1 (工程一館)
Type: academic building, number 8 on the campus map, building code E1.
Location: E1 is the large U-shaped building in the centre of campus, between the Student Activity Center and the central lawn. It surrounds an inner courtyard with a pool. The T4 building is in front of it, towards Keelung Road.
Units in Engineering Building 1:
- Department of Mechanical Engineering (機械工程系)
- Department of Materials Science and Engineering (材料科學與工程系)
