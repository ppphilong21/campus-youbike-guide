First Academic Building (第一教學大樓)
Type: academic building, number 7 on the campus map, building code T1.
Location: T1 is a narrow building near the Keelung Road front, on the Lane 155 side of the Main Gate. It stands between the Athletic Field and the RB building, with the Auditorium beside it.
Units in First Academic Building:
- College of Engineering (工程學院)
- Graduate Institute of Automation and Control (自動化及控制研究所)
