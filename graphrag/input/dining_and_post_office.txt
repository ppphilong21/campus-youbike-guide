Cafeterias and the Post Office at Taiwan Tech

Second Cafeteria of the Student Center (活動中心第二餐廳)
Type: cafeteria, number 20 on the campus map.
Location: The Second Cafeteria is on the side of the Student Activity Center that faces E1, next to the Post Office.

Post Office (郵局)
Type: public facility, number 21 on the campus map.
Location: The Post Office is on the side of the Student Activity Center that faces E1, between the Second Cafeteria and the First Cafeteria.

First Cafeteria of the Student Center (活動中心第一餐廳)
Type: cafeteria, number 22 on the campus map.
Location: The First Cafeteria is at the front corner of the Student Activity Center, near the Volleyball Court and on the path towards T4.

IA Cafeteria (醫揚餐廳)
Type: cafeteria, number 23 on the campus map.
Location: The IA Cafeteria is beside the IA building, next to the Gymnasium on the Lane 155 side.

T2 Cafeteria (第二教學大樓餐廳)
Type: cafeteria, number 24 on the campus map.
Location: The T2 Cafeteria is in the courtyard of the EE-MA-T2 complex, between MA and T2.
