YouBike riding rules and safety

Who can ride: the bicycles are suitable only for riders between 140 cm and 190 cm tall (Article 5.3). The agreement sets no minimum age. A person with limited or no legal capacity may use the service only after their legal representative has read and agreed to the terms (Article 4).

Before riding: check the bike and do not rent it if anything looks abnormal (Article 5.4.11). Do not ride if your physical or mental condition is unfit, and stop riding if it becomes unfit (Article 5.4.1).

On the road:
- Follow the rules for slow vehicles under the Road Traffic Management Penalty Act and the road traffic safety regulations (Article 5.4.2).
- Obey traffic signs, lane markings, signals and police directions (Article 5.4.3).
- Stay out of fast-vehicle lanes and do not cross where crossing is prohibited (Article 5.4.4).
- Stay alert and keep a safe distance from the vehicle in front (Article 5.4.5).
- Brake with the rear brake (right hand) first and then the front brake (left hand), and avoid emergency braking (Article 5.4.6).
- In poor visibility, on slippery roads or on slopes, slow down or walk the bike (Article 5.4.7).
- Do not race, ride abreast in a way that blocks others, or ride dangerously (Article 5.4.8).
- Overtake only where the lane allows passing on the left, then return to your lane (Article 5.4.9).

Not allowed: carrying passengers, overloading or modifying the bike (Article 5.8); carrying dangerous or prohibited items or unsuitable animals (Article 5.8); using the bike for profit or holding it for long periods in a way that inconveniences others (Article 5.4.10); filming or advertising with the bike without written consent (Article 5.9). Riding after drinking is a ground for suspending membership (Article 7.6).

Fines: the rider pays any traffic fines during the rental, as well as towing and storage costs. If the company pays a fine, the member must reimburse it (Article 5.10).
