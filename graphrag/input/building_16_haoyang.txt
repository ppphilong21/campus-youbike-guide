Haoyang Experimental Building (浩揚實驗大樓)
Type: academic building, number 16 on the campus map.
Location: The Haoyang Experimental Building is a small building at the Back Gate on Alley 68, next to the EE building. A GO Station battery swap point and a scooter parking area are beside it, and the official map shows a YouBike 2.0 station just outside the Back Gate.
Units in Haoyang Experimental Building:
- Industry-Academia Innovation College (產學創新學院)
- Taiwan High Speed 3D Printing Research Center (臺灣高速3D列印研究中心)
