EE Building (電資館)
Type: academic building, number 11 on the campus map, building code EE.
Location: EE is the long building along the Alley 68 side of campus, next to the Haoyang Experimental Building and the Back Gate. It forms the back edge of the EE-MA-T2 complex.
Units in EE Building:
- Department of Electronic Engineering (電子工程系)
- Department of Electrical Engineering (電機工程系)
- Graduate Institute of Electro-Optical Engineering (光電工程研究所)
