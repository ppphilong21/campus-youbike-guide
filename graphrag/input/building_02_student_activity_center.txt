Student Activity Center (學生活動中心)
Type: administration building, number 2 on the campus map, building code S.
Location: The Student Activity Center (S) is in the Lane 155 half of the campus, between the Gymnasium and the E1 building. The Second Cafeteria, the Post Office and the First Cafeteria are on its side facing E1, and the Volleyball Court is next to it.
Units in Student Activity Center:
- Office of Student Affairs (學生事務處)
- Indigenous Students Resource Center (原住民族學生資源中心)
