Student and faculty dormitories at Taiwan Tech

1st Student Dormitory (第一學生宿舍)
Type: dormitory, number 26 on the campus map.
Location: The 1st Student Dormitory is on the Alley 68 side of campus, between the dormitory-side Back Gate and International House 1, which is attached to it.

2nd Student Dormitory (第二學生宿舍)
Type: dormitory, number 27 on the campus map.
Location: The 2nd Student Dormitory is on the Alley 68 side of campus, between the 3rd Student Dormitory and the Back Gate near the Haoyang Experimental Building.

3rd Student Dormitory (第三學生宿舍)
Type: dormitory, number 28 on the campus map.
Location: The 3rd Student Dormitory is on the Alley 68 side of campus, between International House 1 and the 2nd Student Dormitory.

1st Faculty Dormitory (職務一舍)
Type: dormitory, number 29 on the campus map.
Location: The 1st Faculty Dormitory is outside the campus wall, across Alley 68 from the student dormitories.

2nd Faculty Dormitory (職務二舍)
Type: dormitory, number 30 on the campus map.
Location: The 2nd Faculty Dormitory is outside the campus wall across Alley 68, between the 1st and 3rd Faculty Dormitories.

3rd Faculty Dormitory (職務三舍)
Type: dormitory, number 31 on the campus map.
Location: The 3rd Faculty Dormitory is outside the campus wall across Alley 68, opposite the Back Gate near the Haoyang Experimental Building.

International House 1 (國際會館一館)
Type: dormitory, number 32 on the campus map.
Location: International House 1 is attached to the 1st Student Dormitory on the Alley 68 side of campus, next to the 3rd Student Dormitory.

International House 2 (國際會館二館)
Type: dormitory, number 33 on the campus map.
Location: International House 2 is near the dormitory-side Back Gate, between the Swimming Pool and the T3 building.

Student dormitory area (學生宿舍區)
Type: dormitory.
Location: The student dormitories (the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Student Dormitories and International House 1) form a row along the Alley 68 side of campus, between the two back gates.
