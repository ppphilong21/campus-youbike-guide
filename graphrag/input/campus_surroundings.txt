Taiwan Tech campus surroundings and YouBike stations on the official map

National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (Taiwan Tech, NTUST) is at No. 43, Keelung Road Section 4, Da'an District, Taipei 10607. On the official campus map the campus is bounded by four roads. Keelung Road Section 4 runs along the front of campus, where the Main Gate is. Lane 155 of Keelung Road Section 3 runs along the side with the Gymnasium, IA and TR buildings. Alley 68 of Lane 41, Keelung Road Section 4 runs along the back of campus, where the Back Gate and the student dormitories are. Lane 41 and Lane 73 of Keelung Road Section 4 run along the side with the EE, T2, E2 and IB buildings. Along Keelung Road, the Lane 155 end leads towards Keelung and the Lane 73 end leads towards Gongguan.

Taiwan Tech Industry 4.0 Implementation Center (工業4.0中心)
Type: research center.
Location: The Taiwan Tech Industry 4.0 Implementation Center is outside the main campus across Lane 41, near Taipei Municipal Minzu Junior High School.

Gong Guan Elementary School (公館國小)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: Gong Guan Elementary School is across Alley 68, behind the campus near the faculty dormitories.

Military Base (軍營)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: A military base is behind the campus, next to the graduate student parking lot at Alley 68 and Lane 41.

Taipei Municipal Minzu Junior High School (民族國中)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: Taipei Municipal Minzu Junior High School is across Lane 41 from the EE and T2 buildings.

Taiwan Power Company Taipei Branch (臺電臺北市區營業處)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: The Taiwan Power Company Taipei Branch is across Lane 73 from the IB building.

NTU Cancer Center (臺大醫院癌症醫療中心)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: The NTU Cancer Center is across Lane 155 from the Gymnasium and the IA building.

NTU Veterinary Hospital (臺大動物醫院)
Type: neighbouring place outside the campus.
Location: The NTU Veterinary Hospital is across Lane 155 from the TR building, near Keelung Road.
