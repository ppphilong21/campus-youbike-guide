Research Building (綜合研究大樓)
Type: academic building, number 13 on the campus map, building code RB.
Location: RB is on the Keelung Road front of campus, on the Lane 155 side of the Main Gate, between T1 and the Library. The official map shows a scooter parking area and a YouBike 2.0 station on Keelung Road in front of it.
Units in Research Building:
- College of Design (設計學院)
- Department of Architecture (建築系)
- Department of Design (設計系)
- Computer Center (電子計算機中心)
- RB-101 Conference Room (RB101會議室)
- RB-102 International Conference Room (國際會議廳)
- RB-105 Lecture Hall (演講廳)
